Who We Are

Mesquite's Installation Specialists

Blue Sky Sales, Inc. is a Mesquite, Texas-based subcontractor and manufacturer's representative. We don't manufacture or build the products we sell — we install them. We're installation specialists, handling the sale, installation, modification, and service ourselves, in-house, from start to finish.

We work directly with general contractors, architects, and property owners on commercial, multifamily, and high-rise projects throughout Mesquite, Garland, Balch Springs, Sunnyvale, and the wider DFW Metroplex.

  • Authorized representative for Wilkinson Hi-Rise, Columbia Chutes, Bilco & Precision Ladders
  • In-house installation crews — not just a supplier
  • Trusted on commercial, multifamily & high-rise projects
  • Real people who answer the phone
