Who We Are

Building DFW, One Expert Installation at a Time

Blue Sky Sales, Inc. is a Texas-based subcontractor and manufacturer's representative serving the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex. We specialize in the sale, installation, modification, and service of building specialty products, managing every project in-house from start to finish.

We partner with general contractors, architects, developers, and property owners to deliver reliable, customized solutions for commercial, multifamily, and high-rise projects.

  • The North Texas representative for Bilco & Precision Ladders, and we work closely with Wilkinson Hi-Rise, Columbia Chutes & other leading manufacturers
  • In-house installation crews — not just a supplier
  • Trusted on commercial, multifamily & high-rise projects
  • Real people who answer the phone
