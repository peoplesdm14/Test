Who We Are

A Mesquite-Built Company, Raised On Referrals

Blue Sky Sales, Inc. is a family-owned general contracting company based in Mesquite, Texas. We've built our reputation the old-fashioned way — straight talk, fair pricing, and crews who show up when they say they will. Whether it's a ground-up build, a full remodel, or a roof that needs to survive the next Texas hailstorm, our team treats every job site like it's our own home.

We work directly with homeowners, property owners, and local businesses throughout Mesquite, Garland, Balch Springs, Sunnyvale, and the wider DFW Metroplex.

  • Transparent, itemized estimates — no surprise change orders
  • In-house crews and vetted local subcontractors
  • Clean, safe, respectful job sites from day one to walkthrough
  • Real people who answer the phone
